Questions

Is my logo stored?

It is processed on the server and the generated pack is deleted within 24 hours. Nothing is kept beyond that, and nothing is used for anything else.

What do I get in the download?

A zip with the trimmed original, a white variant for dark backgrounds, the isolated square mark in both colors, a rounded app badge, palette.json with the sampled hexes, and every generated mockup.

The palette looks wrong. What now?

Click any swatch to adjust it, or remove swatches that came from anti aliasing noise. The confirm step exists exactly for this.

Can it do Arabic brands?

Yes. The extraction is script agnostic and the full skill builds complete RTL Arabic decks. Wordmarks with Arabic letters are composited from your real PNG, never redrawn by AI.

What powers the mockups?

gpt-image-1 image edits with your exact mark passed as a reference at high input fidelity, so the letters survive. Generation is rate limited to keep the service free.
