What you get

Everything a design agency would charge thousands for.

Guidelines deck

10 to 45 landscape slides as one clean HTML file, exported to a print ready PDF. Cover, contents, dark dividers, page numbers, the whole ritual.

Logo system

Wordmark, symbol and app badge with white and dark variants, clear space rules and misuse examples. Bring your own logo or get 6 to 8 designed options.

Color and type

A palette with roles and HEX, RGB and CMYK values, plus a display and body pairing with specimens. Bilingual and Arabic script support included.

Real mockups

How it works

Four steps from nothing to a finished identity.

Brief it

Tell Claude the brand name, market, audience and vibe, or compose it with the builder below. Have a logo? Hand it over and it stays untouched.

Lock the foundations

The skill samples or designs the palette, picks the type pairing and confirms the logo direction with you before building thirty plus slides. Reversing later is expensive, so it asks first.

Generate real mockups

Your logo is passed to the image model as a reference so products carry the genuine mark. Guard prompts block redrawn letters and invented gibberish text.

Review and export

The deck prints to PDF through headless Chrome, then every single page is rasterized and visually inspected before it reaches you.

Questions

Asked often.

What does it actually create?

A complete brand guidelines deck: logo system with variants and rules, a full color system with HEX, RGB and CMYK, a typography pairing with specimens, voice and values, photography direction, and real AI photographed product mockups, all exported to PDF.

Is it free?

Yes, MIT licensed. The deck renders free with headless Chrome. The optional photo mockups run on your own keys: GEMINI_API_KEY for logo bearing products and OPENAI_API_KEY for illustrations and patterns.

Can I use my existing logo?

Yes, and that is the most common mode. The skill samples the palette from your logo pixels, builds white and dark variants, isolates the symbol for badges, and never redraws your letters.

Does it speak Arabic?

Yes. Right to left decks with proper Arabic typography, bilingual lockups and fully localized guidelines are built in.

What do I need?

Claude Code, Google Chrome and Python 3 with Pillow. That is the whole stack.
